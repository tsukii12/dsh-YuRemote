# YuRemote branding and artwork · 鱼鱼远程品牌与素材

The app uses YuRemote branding and the illustration supplied by Tsuki. The app and in-app icons are based on that illustration. The upstream dsh-Go name and logo and its mascot artwork are not included as YuRemote branding: [dsh-Go’s asset policy](https://github.com/ccch1mneyyy/dsh-Go/blob/c1a84e3f833ad59ff07a5027994dae3935907a2d/ASSETS.md) excludes these from its AGPL grant. References to dsh-Go in credits identify the source, not the product.

See [NOTICE](NOTICE) for provenance and [THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md) for third-party code, fonts and assets. The [AGPL-3.0 license](LICENSE) applies to the app code; consult the notices for material with separate terms.
